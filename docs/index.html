Franka arm: RL to vision (2 min 15 s)

One video for two projects. A simulated Franka arm in Isaac Lab learns to lift a cube and hold it at a target point with reinforcement learning (PPO, exact state), one difficulty at a time. That expert then teaches a camera-only student by imitation (behaviour cloning, then DAgger), which sees only two camera images and its own joint readings. Simulation only; no real robot.

Success in 1,000-episode tests (cube ends within 3 cm of the target): expert reading exact state 99.5 %; camera-only student 95.1 %, and 90.2 % with random colours and lighting; best modular vision pipeline 66.0 %. With colours and lighting never seen in training the student drops to 46.9 %. Download the mp4.
